About

A Software engineer

who found his footing in data.

I move between SQL Server, Power BI, Excel and Python depending on what a problem actually needs — writing T-SQL and stored procedures to get data out cleanly, building ETL workflows with SSIS, and turning the result into SSRS reports or Power BI dashboards that a business can act on.

What I care about most is that the numbers are right before they're pretty. Most of my hands-on training has centered on data validation, reconciliation, and schema comparison.

Player Performance Hub — Real Madrid 2015/16

Personal project · Power BI
What it demonstrates

A sports analytics dashboard built on public season data, used to practice modeling and visualizing performance metrics outside a typical business dataset.

The Player Performance Hub page ranks top scorers and assist providers, tracks minutes played and disciplinary record per player, and calculates efficiency metrics like goals-per-90 and conversion rate. A second page, Team Performance Overview, extends the analysis to team-level results.
